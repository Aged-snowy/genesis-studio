Privacy: what is kept and why

Without an account

Everything happens on your own device. The files you open are read by the page in your browser and are never sent anywhere. Your library is saved only in your browser on this device. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

With an account

Accounts are optional. If you make one, these are the only things kept online:

  • Your email address, used only to sign in and to send you links to confirm your account or reset your password. It is never shown to anyone, sold or shared.
  • A protected form of your password called a salted hash. Your real password is never stored, so nobody, not even the people who run Genesis Studio, can read it.
  • The blueprints you save to your library, and your library name.
  • The usual sign in records kept by the account service, such as when you last signed in, to protect your account from attacks.

The files you open are still never uploaded. Only the finished blueprints in your library are saved online.

The database only ever lets you see or change your own blueprints. Every connection is encrypted.

Your choices

  • Use Genesis Studio without an account. Everything still works.
  • Download a copy of your data at any time under Your account, then Download or delete my data.
  • Delete your account at any time in the same place. Your account and every blueprint kept online are removed for good.

Who runs it

Genesis and Genesis Studio are made by PixelProphetOG. Accounts are provided through Supabase, which stores the account data on secure servers.
