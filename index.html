GENESIS

Speak it, and it is built.

Genesis is a Minecraft Bedrock add on that builds whatever you describe, and Version 7 turns your world into a living story: a ten act Chronicle told by the Prophet, sixteen metals and a Forge, a grammar of magic, machines and flux, forty two creatures, three realms, farms and kitchens, and towns that grow into kingdoms. This studio turns pictures, 3D models and schematics into blueprints that Genesis builds for you, block by block.

1 Get Genesis

Download Genesis and open it. Minecraft opens and installs it. Then make or edit a world, and under Behavior Packs turn on Genesis. Type /build and describe anything.

2 Turn anything into blocks

Drop a file here, or

Almost any file. Pictures and photos of every common kind (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, ICO, SVG, AVIF, TIFF, Photoshop PSD, Targa, QOI, Netpbm), 3D models (OBJ, STL, GLB, glTF, PLY, OFF, 3MF, Collada, Blockbench, Bedrock geometry, MagicaVoxel), schematics (Litematica, WorldEdit, old MCEdit schematics, structure files, mcstructure) and Minecraft command files. Videos give you five moments to choose from. Music and sounds become pictures of how they sound. PDFs give you each page. Zip, tar and gzip archives, add on packs and Office documents are opened and searched. Fonts, spreadsheets and plain writing work too, and anything else becomes a pattern of its own colors. For an OBJ with colors, choose its .mtl and texture files at the same time.

3 Send it to Minecraft

Your library is empty. Add something in step 2.

    1. Open the saved .mcpack file. Minecraft imports it.
    2. In your world settings, under Behavior Packs, turn on Genesis Library next to Genesis.
    3. Load the world. Genesis says how many blueprints arrived.
    4. /build paste name builds one in front of you. /build overlay name guides you block by block. /build materials name lists what you need.

    5 Use it as an app

    Genesis Studio works in any modern web browser on Windows, Mac, ChromeOS, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad. You can also install it like an app. It then opens in its own window, gets its own icon, and keeps working without the internet once it has opened one time.

    Genesis Studio is installed on this device. You can open it from your apps.

    • Windows, Mac, ChromeOS and Linux: in Chrome or Edge, press the Install button above. You can also open the browser menu and choose Install Genesis Studio, or Save and share, then Install page as app.
    • Mac with Safari: open the File menu and choose Add to Dock.
    • Android: press the Install button above, or open the Chrome menu and choose Add to Home screen, then Install.
    • iPhone and iPad: in Safari, press the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
